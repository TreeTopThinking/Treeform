# Grammar
Treeform has a few easy to learn grammar rules.
### Punctuation
- **Periods** are put at the end of **statements**
- **Exclamation marks** are at the end of **emphasized statements**
- **Question marks** are at the end of **questions**
- **Commas** are used to separate between **breaths or ideas**
### Capitalization
- The first letter of **every word** is capitalized
- When spoken the syllables with capitalized letters should be **noticeably more emphasized than the syllables without it**
### Pluralization
- When the plural operator is placed before a word it indicates that the following word has a quantity greater than one
## Sentence Structure
- Operators are placed before the word they modify
- Concepts, relations and states can be logically connected to create sentences 
- [examples.csv](examples.csv) shows sentence examples
